# The Players tab: js/players.js

Part of the repo's notes: CLAUDE.md says which file covers what and how to keep them current. Read this before changing js/players.js.

- players: Players tab (`#players`, was Free Agents): one table (`topPerformancesCard`) of every NBA stat line of
  a menu of Today / Yesterday (the last game day) / This period (once it has two game days) / Last period (range 'last') / This season (football: Projected first), and for a period or the season a Totals / Per game toggle (`ui.perfPer`; the season per game is `seasonRows`, averages from the Stats used basis, internally range 'avg'); top 300 for the season ranges, 100 otherwise, ranked by value (pickup ideas are only on Plan Ahead); games (GP, past one day) and minutes (MIN) are columns of their own after the name (`perfCols`; none when the league scores MIN or GP itself), and under the name is only whose he is (and a game on now), one line on phones too, a long team's name cut with "…"; the last column reads VAL, a positive value with no "+" (the owner's ask, Nov 23, 2026: games and minutes under the name made phone rows four or five lines tall);
  search, then one Filters button (`ui.perfFiltOpen`) opening a panel (and the tab's one (i), beside it: `INFO.perf`, kept to what no tip on screen says, short enough for a phone; Per game's line names its stats with no (i) of its own: the owner's ask, Nov 20, 2026; on phones, ≤640px, neither this (i) nor the line of days, update time and player count shows: "too messy", the owner's ask, Nov 21, 2026): Show toggles (`ui.perfWhose`: me, opp, other, fa, any together, never none; Other teams is also a menu of those teams to pick any number of, the owner's ask: `perfOtherMenu`, `ui.perfOthers`, none picked = all), Lineup All / Active / Bench (`ui.perfSpot`, rostered players only: waivers have no lineup), position and NBA team (each a menu to pick any number from, the owner's ask: `perfMenu`, lists `ui.perfPos` / `ui.perfTeam`, empty = all; a player at any picked position), Rookies, Injured.
- A row's injury (O out, D day-to-day: one letter, the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026) and rookie (a blue R, the owner's pick) badges always sit
  on the name's line, phones too, the name cut with "…" to make room (the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026; before, they dropped to the line under a
  cut name, as a fantasy team's card still does: `fitNameBadges`, render.js).
- A rostered row's lineup spot is one small dot by its team's name (`lineupBadge` in `perfOwners`), never a word: filled starter, hollow bench,
  red-ringed injured reserve, dashed taxi (the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026: IR and Taxi were badges, the only spots with a word).
