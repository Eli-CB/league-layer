# Player and team cards

Part of the repo's notes: CLAUDE.md says which file covers what and how to keep them current. Read this before changing the player card or a team's card (core.js, players.js, views.js).

## The player card
- The card's title: his name, then team, positions, flag, years in the league; no jersey number, and his age is in `cardBio` beside his weight (the owner's ask, Nov 20, 2026).
- The player card: `pc-side` (the photo, `cardBio` under it: height, weight, age, hockey's shooting hand, then "Drafted 9th in 2021" or "Undrafted"; no college, the owner's ask, Nov 20, 2026, so the jobs no longer write `col`; the daily jobs' `ht`/`wt`/`dft`/`sh` from NBA.com's player list, NHL.com, Sleeper), `pc-who` (fantasy team and how he got there, then `catProfile`, the owner's ask, Nov 2026: a bar a category against an average rostered player, goalies against goalies, in spreads over BASE's own players `BASE.stats`; points leagues each scored stat's FP; not football), `pc-tiles` (with "Playoff games", the owner's ask, Nov 20, 2026: his team's, as on its card, `playoffSlate`; a row of its own on phones), `pc-log` (averages and games on one column grid: Season + GP as wide as the games' one Game column), games sortable (`card-sort`, `ui.cardSort`), each game "20/11 @ DET" (day/month, as the owner wrote it) green won, red lost, by how much on hover (`gameTxt`; the owner's ask, Nov 23, 2026: date, matchup and W/L were three columns; the room went to basketball's PF, after TO; the daily file's few games before the box scores load have no PF: —), picture `playerPhoto` (the full cutout, NBA.com's for basketball, ESPN's by `espn` id otherwise, in front of the team logo; the round photo if it won't load).
- Player card: under his fantasy team, how he got there (`cardHow`: Keeper / Drafted round, pick / Pickup / Traded from, with the week, from the weekly rosters; ESPN: the draft only)

- Fantasy team names in the cards (the player card's team and its "Traded from" team: `cardHow`'s `out.from`, `howBadge`; a team card's opponent; a sport team card's owners) are `teamLink`s, with no underline (the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026: one was tried for phones and dropped).

## A sport team's card
- `teamBtn(tri)` / `linkTeams(html)` open it ('sport-team', `showSportTeamCard` in players.js; its record from `teamRecord`: the box scores' finals, then today's once over on ESPN's scoreboard; hockey W-L-OT from each final's `last`; checked against ESPN's records; then, the owner's ask, "Playoff Games: 3-4-4-3-4", its games in each of the league's playoff periods still to play, `playoffSlate` in nba.js, a row of its own on phones). On phones its game tiles are drawn as the player card's (`:is(.pc-tiles, .tc-tiles)` in styles.css): Next game as wide as its game on one line ("Wed vs MEM" spilled out of a third of a 360 px phone, and the team card's broke as "Sat vs / POR", Nov 20, 2026), the other two its labels' two words a line.
- No players table (the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026: the Players tab says it better): first the link to the Players tab filtered by the team ('team-players', at the top, the owner's ask), the tiles (Games next period only when Games ahead doesn't show, as its first period), then `sportScheduleHtml` (Games ahead: the next 5 periods' games, green or red against the average team's, which shows on hover alone (the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026: no "avg" in the tile, no subtitle), `teamDates`, back-to-backs; not football), `sportOutHtml` (Who's out: the injury report, and for a regular (20+ minutes, hockey 15) who missed 3+ games, the two teammates whose average game (`lineValue`) rose most without him, 3+ games each side, from the box scores; redrawn once they load), `sportFreeHtml` (its 3 best players on no fantasy team, and how many are rostered; a league only).

## A fantasy team's card
- A fantasy team's name anywhere (`teamLink`, core.js; plain in past seasons and inside menus) opens its card in the player card's dialog (`showTeamCard`/`teamCardHtml`, `teamCardTitle`): Category strengths in full (`teamStrengths`, any team's, points leagues too, not football; moved from Standings Nov 2026, the owner's ask: number, league average, bar, rank, every team's dot, the card's team the big one and yours orange, a row opening to who helped and hurt), counting each team's top N (N = its lineup's spots, IR left out: an end-of-bench player or a one-off start doesn't move them), in a roto league under the team's real standing (`rotoCardStanding`: its totals, points and the teams either side, Nov 24, 2026: the roster's ranks alone read as its place in the standings, which can be far apart) and headed "Roster by category". No roster table (the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026: the Players tab says it better). Top to bottom: the link to the Players tab showing that team's players alone ('fantasy-team-players': Show narrowed to Mine, Opponent or that one Other team; at the top, the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026), as an NBA team's card has ('team-players'; both through `showOnPlayers`, actions.js), and Compare on Plan Ahead; `teamTopHtml` (its 4 best players as tiles); `teamWeeksHtml` (Season so far: a chip a finished regular-season week, W/L and its score from the starters' box scores, then its all-play record and its luck, only when it's lucky or unlucky by half a win or more (the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026): `allPlayRecords`, Best manager's counting; Fantrax and Sleeper only, not roto); the category strengths; `teamBuildHtml` (How it was built: each player grouped by `cardHow`'s line, a row a group drawn by `tcHowRow` (the landing preview's too: the player card's badge from `HOW_BADGE` and the count, the photos overlapped best first, 6 then "+N", the names under them; the owner's ask, Nov 27, 2026), then its best pickup and draft pick so far, Awards' `pickupImpact`/`draftImpact`). Season so far and How it was built need this season's weeks (`loadCurSeason`/`curSeasonLeague`, history.js, shared with Awards so far): a loading line marked `data-tc-wait` until they're in, then `teamCardWait` draws the card again if it's still open. A name and its comma share a `.tc-nw` span: a button stays inline-block whatever its CSS says, so a comma after one could start a line. Both team cards (this one and an NBA team's) rank and show each player's value over the season, `seasonTotal` (nba.js: this season's average game × its games), not a game's, so a game or two can't top them.
